About Oil-Twin AI

Oil-Twin AI is an interactive digital twin, training, and nodal analysis engineering platform designed for petroleum production engineers, reservoir analysts, and students.

Key Physics Foundations:

  • IPR (Inflow Performance Relationship): Darcy linear model above bubble point ($P_b$), Vogel two-phase quadratic model below bubble point.
  • VLP (Vertical Lift Performance): Modified Beggs & Brill and Ansari multiphase correlations accounting for liquid holdup, mixture density, and friction loss across Bubble, Slug, Churn, and Mist regimes.
  • Choke Hydraulics: Gilbert and Sachdeva multiphase orifice equations evaluating critical vs subcritical flow cutoffs ($P_{down}/P_{up} \le 0.546$).
  • Artificial Lift: Continuous Gas Lift (gradient relief vs over-injection friction) and Multi-Stage ESP (Affinity laws $Q \propto f, H \propto f^2, P \propto f^3$, free gas derating).
  • Sucker-Rod Pumping: Kinematic beam pump linkage motion, rod string elasticity, and surface/downhole dynamometer card diagnostics.
Technical Reference:

Brown, K.E., "The Technology of Artificial Lift Methods", PennWell Books; Beggs, H.D. & Brill, J.P., "A Study of Two-Phase Flow in Inclined Pipes", JPT.
